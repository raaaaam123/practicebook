Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PracticeBook / BookNest, operated by SHIVAM PRAJAPATI, aims to provide a fair and transparent refund and cancellation process. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Cancellation Policy

Due to the digital nature of our products (e-books and educational materials), orders are generally processed and delivered immediately after payment confirmation. Once a digital product has been delivered to you (i.e., download access has been provided), the order cannot be canceled.

If an order has been placed but the product has not yet been delivered, you may request a cancellation by contacting us at Practicekaro.it@gmail.com.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be considered under the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate payment: If you were charged more than once for the same order, we will refund the excess amount.
  • Product not delivered: If payment was successful but you did not receive access to the purchased digital product, we will either provide access or issue a full refund.
  • Defective or corrupted product: If the digital product you received is corrupted, incomplete, or significantly different from its description, we will work to resolve the issue or provide a refund.
  • Failed payment: If your payment was deducted but the order was not placed successfully, the amount will be automatically reversed by your bank within 5-10 business days. If not, please contact us.

3. Refund Process

To request a refund, please email us at Practicekaro.it@gmail.com with the following details:

  • Your name
  • Order details (date of purchase, product name)
  • Reason for the refund request
  • Payment transaction ID or receipt

We will review your request and respond within 5-7 business days. If approved, the refund will be processed to the original payment method within 7-10 business days.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Refund requests made after the digital product has been successfully delivered and accessed
  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Requests made after 7 days from the date of purchase (unless required by law)
  • Products purchased during promotional sales (unless the product is defective)

5. Failed Payments

If your payment fails or is declined, no amount will be charged to your account. If an amount is deducted but the order is not confirmed, it will be automatically reversed by your bank within 5-10 business days. If the amount is not reversed within this period, please contact us with your transaction details.

6. Duplicate Payments

If you accidentally made a duplicate payment for the same product, please contact us at Practicekaro.it@gmail.com with your transaction details. We will verify and refund the duplicate amount within 7-10 business days.
